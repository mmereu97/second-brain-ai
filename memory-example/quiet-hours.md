metadata:
  type: feedback

No notifications on Sunday between 09:00 and 12:00, and none at night (22:00–07:00) unless explicitly asked (e.g. for medication).
**Why:** a notification during those hours is worse than a late one.
**How to apply:** schedule outside the window; `ntfy-send` refuses those times anyway.
